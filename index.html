About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science in a joint program between EURECOM, Sorbonne University (EDITE doctoral school) and the Huawei Paris Research Center. My research focuses on the network side of large-scale AI training: making collective communication libraries (CCLs) adapt to real network conditions, from GPU clusters inside a datacenter to training that spans multiple datacenters over the WAN. In my PhD I work on enhancing AI clusters with holistic monitoring systems.

Before my PhD, I earned a double M.Sc. degree in Networks & Cloud Computing and Data Science from Politecnico di Torino and EURECOM, and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino. I am a member of IEEE-HKN, the honor society of the IEEE, and of the ACM.
